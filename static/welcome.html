Keep the tabs that matter alive

Chrome's Memory Saver unloads idle tabs to free RAM, then reloads them when you return. Keep Tabs Alive lets you choose which tabs stay active and ready — preventing unwanted reloads and interruptions.

Tabs stay put

Open tabs stay ready to use, with their page state and ongoing work intact — no unexpected reloads.

Per-tab switches

The settings page lists every open tab with its own switch. Keep one tab alive or let another sleep — it overrides the rules just for that tab.

Site rules

Protect everything except a few sites, or whitelist only the ones you choose. Rules match subdomains too — example.com covers all of it.

Prefer to protect everything by default? You can switch modes in settings.
