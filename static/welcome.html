Keep the tabs that matter alive

Chrome saves resources two ways: it unloads tabs you aren't using, and it slows down tabs you can't see. Keep Tabs Alive stops each — tab by tab, only where you want it.

Keep in memory

Chrome won't unload the tab.

Come back to it and everything is exactly where you left it — no reload.

  • Typed text, drafts and forms stay
  • Free: no extra battery
  • While hidden, the page may still slow down

Good for: long forms, documents, mail, dashboards you check now and then

Keep running

The page keeps working at full speed — even when you can't see it.

Chrome treats the tab as in use, so nothing in it pauses while you're elsewhere.

  • Chats, AI answers, timers, music and live data keep going
  • Off by itself on a timer, if you like
  • Uses more battery; Chrome shows its screen-sharing icon — nothing is recorded

Good for: chats and AI tools, trading and monitoring, timers, uploads

+

Need both? “Keep alive” turns on both modes for a tab in one click.

How to turn them on

  1. 1

    Pin it for one-click access: click the 🧩 puzzle icon in the toolbar, then the pin.

  2. 2

    On any tab, click the icon — the window has a switch for each mode.

  3. 3

    In Settings see every open tab, and keep whole sites in memory.

Nothing leaves your browser: no tracking, no servers. Keep running never records the page.
