Keep the tabs that matter

Chrome quietly unloads idle tabs to save memory, then reloads them when you come back — losing your scroll position, forms and state. Tell Keep Tabs Alive which sites should always stay alive, and it leaves the rest to Chrome.

Tabs stay put

No more surprise reloads. Open tabs keep their scroll position, unsaved forms and page state exactly where you left them.

Per-tab switches

The settings page lists every open tab with its own switch. Keep one tab alive or let another sleep — it overrides the rules just for that tab.

Site rules

Protect everything except a few sites, or whitelist only the ones you choose. Rules match subdomains too — example.com covers all of it.

Prefer to protect everything by default? You can switch modes in settings.
